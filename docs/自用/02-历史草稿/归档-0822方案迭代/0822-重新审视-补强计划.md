## 1. 重新审视 L2 公开数据集结果

### 1.1 当前数据的**真实优势**（被我之前忽略的）

#### **Storage-matched 对照是决定性证据**

| Baseline | Storage | Hit (LME-S) | 与 SQCAD 的 paired 差异 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BM25 (full store)** | 74,092 | 0.967 | +0.213 (显著) |
| **BM25-recency-12** | ~1,200 | 0.065 | **-0.690 [-0.730, -0.644]** (显著) |
| **BM25-stream (online)** | 1,631 | 0.363 | **-0.392 [-0.445, -0.336]** (显著) |
| **SQCAD (frozen)** | 1,631 | 0.754 | — |

**这说明什么？**
- 在 **相同存储预算** 下，SQCAD 的 hit 0.754 **显著高于**：
  - BM25-recency-12 的 0.065（**11.6× 提升**）
  - BM25-stream 的 0.363（**2.1× 提升**）
  - dense-stream 的 0.229（**3.3× 提升**）

**这是实质性的优势，不是"trade-off"，而是在约束下的优越性！**

---

#### **Guard 机制的精确价值**

| Strategy | Storage | Hit | Exposure |
|---|---|---|---|
| SQCAD | 1,631 | 0.754 | 2,722 |
| + Guard-1 | 1,631 | 0.902 | 2,712 |
| + Guard-4 | 1,631 | **0.944** | 2,654 |
| BM25 (full) | 74,092 | 0.967 | 1,973 |

**Guard-4 在相同存储下达到 BM25 95.6% 的 hit（0.944 vs 0.967），仅差 -0.023**

而且：
- Storage: 1,631 vs 74,092（**45× 压缩**）
- Exposure: 2,654 vs 1,973（+34.5%，但这是 one-shot workspace 的增量）

**这不是"未超越"，这是在 1/45 存储预算下接近 full-store 的质量！**

## 6. Rebuttal 准备（3 个最可能的问题）

### Q1：理论贡献是否足够新？

**预判**：Reviewer 可能说 "这只是 POMDP + Blackwell 的 Agent-memory 应用"

**防御**：
> "We agree that Bellman/Blackwell are standard tools. Our contribution is three-fold:
> 
> (1) **Lifecycle contrast decomposition** (Theorem 1): separating immediate utility, 
>     candidate/workspace access effects, and conditional evidence experiments — 
>     L1 ablation verifies that removing censoring changes lifecycle slope from 5.85 
>     to 0.044 (§5.1), a 133× reduction.
> 
> (2) **Score sufficiency iff lifecycle-value measurability** (Theorem 2): the exact 
>     condition when a current score preserves all cost-shifted decisions, with 
>     constructive witnesses showing identical local effects (2.0) yielding opposite 
>     lifecycle signs (±1776 vs ±1784, §5.1).
> 
> (3) **Action-dependent transcript KL budget** (Theorem 3b): which Agent actions 
>     carry distinguishing information when archive censors future evidence — 
>     verified by the self-censoring slope 5.8500 matching τp(T-n_early)/T exactly.
> 
> The math tools are standard; the lifecycle control problem, its falsifiable score 
> sufficiency condition, and the censoring-regret consequence are new."

---

### Q2：L2 结果为何不直接超越 full-store baseline？

**预判**：Reviewer 可能说 "SQCAD hit 0.754 < BM25 0.967，为什么要用？"

**防御**：
